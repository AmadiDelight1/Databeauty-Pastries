Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Butter or margarine, softened
  • 3/4 cup Sugar
  • 3/4 cup Brown sugar, packed
  • 2 Eggs, large
  • 2 teaspoons Vanilla
  • 2 1/4 cups Flour, unsifted
  • 1 teaspoon Baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon Salt
  • 1 cup Walnuts or pecans, chopped (optional)
  • 2 cups KCC Bitter sweet 60% Cacao Baking Chips

Baking Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 375°F
  2. Stir flour with baking soda and salt; set aside.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, beat butter, sugar, and brown sugar at medium speed until creamy and lightened in color.
  4. Add eggs and vanilla, one at a time. Mix on low speed until incorporated.
  5. Gradually blend the dry mixture into the creamed mixture.
  6. Stir in nuts and chocolate chips.
  7. Drop dough by tablespoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheets.
  8. Bake for 9–11 minutes or until the cookies are golden brown.
  9. In a large mixing bowl, beat butter, sugar, and brown suger.
